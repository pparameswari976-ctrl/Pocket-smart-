# PocketSmart - Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Idea
PocketSmart is an AI-powered budget assistant.

## Problem Statement
People often find it difficult to track daily expenses and manage their monthly budget.

## Proposed Solution
PocketSmart aims to help users organize their expenses and plan their budget.

## Target Users
Students, families, and people who want to manage their expenses.

## Key Features
- Budget planning
- Expense tracking
- Monthly spending summary
- Budget management suggestions

## Expected Outcome
To help users understand their spending habits and manage their money effectively.
